Narrative Structure Slide 02

IR Frame

Placeholder format for market story, traction, and future confidence.

  • Lead: One sentence headline that sets the investment case.
  • Proof: Metrics, retention, growth, or operating leverage.
  • Close: What the audience should believe next.

PR Frame

Placeholder format for public narrative, product signal, and timing.

  • Lead: Story angle that is clear in one editorial line.
  • Proof: Product moment, customer outcome, or cultural signal.
  • Close: Why this matters now and who it is for.

HR Frame

Placeholder format for mission, team density, and role attraction.

  • Lead: Why the company and why this stage is unusual.
  • Proof: Team quality, speed, craft, and operating principles.
  • Close: What kind of person should lean in.
Keep each page to one idea and one visual rhythm. Replace these blocks with actual narrative later.
Page Composition Slide 03

Suggested content rhythm

Build each deck around a simple sequence: signal, context, proof, and forward motion. This keeps the presentation adaptable across investor, press, and hiring conversations without changing the overall structure.

01 Signal: a clean opening statement
02 Context: why this matters now
03 Proof: evidence and confidence

What to keep consistent

  • Cadence: One primary headline per page, minimal supporting text.
  • Visuals: Strong margins, one focal element, one supporting cluster.
  • Motion: Slides should glide, not snap or hard-cut.
  • Reuse: Same format can flex across IR, PR, and HR conversations.
This file is intended as the shared master format. The deck loops seamlessly when you move past the last slide.
